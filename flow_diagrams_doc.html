2. The 5-Tier Zero-Waste Produce Cascade Flow (< 3% Net Spoilage)

To conquer perishable shrinkage, fresh produce moves through an automated algorithmic devaluation and repurposing funnel. Unsold items from Day 1 are discounted or converted into gourmet value-add kitchen packs.
